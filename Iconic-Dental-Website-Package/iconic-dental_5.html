Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Iconic Dental is a global digital dental design lab. We provide outsourced CAD design services to dental laboratories worldwide — fixed and removable prosthesis, implants, night guards, cosmetic wax-ups and models — so your bench can produce more, faster.

Our designers work in leading CAD platforms including 3Shape and exocad, and we accept scan files from all major intraoral scanners — including .STL, .PLY and .DCM. If you use a different workflow, contact us and we'll confirm compatibility.

Standard cases are returned within 24–48 hours, and rush service is available when you need it same day. Our team is available around the clock to keep your cases moving.

We offer reviews until the design is right — our redesign factor sits under 2%, but if something needs adjusting we handle it quickly at no extra hassle to your team.

Send us your first case through the contact form or reach out by phone, WhatsApp or email. We'll set up your workflow and have your first design back to you in no time.
